Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

InvoiceFollow ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Invoice details (client name, amount, due date, work description) — used only to generate emails
  • Your name (used in generated emails)
  • Email address (if you contact us or subscribe)
  • Payment information (processed by third-party payment providers — we never see your card details)

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • IP address (for security and analytics)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate payment reminder emails you request
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To improve the Service
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To prevent fraud and abuse

4. Data Storage

Invoice details you enter are sent to our AI provider to generate emails. We do not permanently store your invoice details on our servers. Generated emails are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • AI provider (Groq) — for email generation
  • Payment processor (Razorpay / Lemon Squeezy) — for handling payments
  • Hosting (Vercel) — for serving the website

These providers have their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage to track your free usage limit. We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as required to provide the Service (e.g., payment processing) or as required by law.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access information we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.
