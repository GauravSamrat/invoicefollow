Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Introduction

InvoiceFollow ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Invoice data (client name, amount, due date, notes) — used only to display and manage your invoices
  • Your name and email (if you sign up)
  • Payment transaction IDs from Razorpay (for credit purchases — we never see or store card details)

Stored only in your browser (never on our servers):

  • AI provider API keys — if you use Bring Your Own Key (BYOK), your key is stored in your browser's localStorage and sent directly to your chosen AI provider when you generate a message. We never receive, see, or store your API key.
  • Guest-mode invoices and settings — until you sign up, all your data stays in your browser only

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • IP address (for security and analytics)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To display and manage your invoices and reminders
  • To process credit purchases and manage your account
  • To improve the Service
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To prevent fraud and abuse

4. Data Storage

Guest-mode data (invoices, settings, AI credits) is stored only in your browser's localStorage. Signed-in user data is stored securely in Supabase.

When you use AI features, the prompt you send (including invoice details and client name) is transmitted to your chosen AI provider to generate a response. We do not permanently store the prompts or responses on our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

InvoiceFollow uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — for account authentication and invoice storage (if signed up)
  • Vercel — for hosting the website and serverless functions
  • Razorpay — for processing credit purchases
  • AI providers (optional): Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, OpenRouter — used only when you trigger an AI task

Each provider has its own privacy policy governing their use of your data.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to:

  • Track your free AI credits
  • Store your invoices (guest mode)
  • Store your API keys (BYOK mode)
  • Remember your preferences and settings

We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking. We do not sell your data to third parties.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • When necessary to provide the Service (e.g., payment processing via Razorpay)
  • When you explicitly trigger an AI task (prompt sent to AI provider)
  • When required by law

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access information we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data as CSV at any time
  • Opt out of any communications
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. AI Provider Disclosure

When you use an AI feature (like "Generate with AI" for a reminder), the text of your prompt — including invoice details and client name — is sent to your chosen AI provider. This is necessary for the AI to generate a response.

  • If you use your own API key (BYOK): prompts are sent to your chosen provider using your key. We do not see the request or the response.
  • If you use InvoiceFollow's default credits: prompts are sent to Groq (or another backend AI provider we use) via our servers.

We recommend reviewing your AI provider's privacy policy to understand how they handle the data you send.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
