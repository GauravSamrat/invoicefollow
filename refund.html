Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Our Promise

We want you to be completely satisfied with InvoiceFollow. If the Service does not meet your expectations, we offer a straightforward refund policy.

2. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may request a full refund within 7 days of your initial payment.

  • Applies to both the Pro monthly plan and the Lifetime plan
  • No questions asked
  • Refund processed within 5-7 business days

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at invoicefollow@gmail.com with:

  • Your name
  • Email used for purchase
  • Payment/transaction ID (if available)
  • Reason for refund (optional but helpful)

4. After 7 Days

Refunds will not be issued after the 7-day window. However, you may cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future charges.

5. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your Pro subscription at any time:

  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle
  • You keep access to Pro features until the cycle ends
  • No further charges after cancellation
  • To cancel, email us at invoicefollow@gmail.com

6. Exceptions

Refunds may not be issued in cases of:

  • Abuse of the Service (spam, fraud, violation of Terms)
  • Repeated refund requests from the same user
  • Requests made more than 7 days after payment

7. Failed or Duplicate Payments

If you were charged twice or your payment failed but was deducted, contact us immediately. We will investigate and issue a full refund within 5-7 business days.

8. Lifetime Plan Refunds

The Lifetime plan is also covered by our 7-day money-back guarantee. After 7 days, Lifetime purchases are non-refundable.
