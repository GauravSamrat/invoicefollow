Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Our Promise

InvoiceFollow uses a credit-based model. You pay for what you use. We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not, we offer a straightforward refund policy.

2. Credit Purchases — 7-Day Refund Window

Unused credits can be refunded within 7 days of your purchase date. Conditions:

  • Credits must be unused — partially used packs are not refundable
  • Refund is for the full amount originally paid
  • Refund processed within 5-7 business days

3. Pro Subscription Refunds

If you subscribed to the Pro plan and are not satisfied:

  • You may request a full refund within 7 days of your first payment
  • After 7 days, refunds are not available for the current month
  • You can cancel anytime — cancellation prevents future charges

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at invoicefollow@gmail.com with:

  • Your full name and registered email
  • Transaction ID / Razorpay payment ID
  • Date of purchase
  • Reason for refund (optional but helpful)

5. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your Pro subscription anytime:

  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle
  • You keep Pro access until the cycle ends
  • No further charges after cancellation
  • Your remaining credits (if any) can still be used after cancellation

6. Exceptions — No Refund

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • Credits that have already been used (partially or fully)
  • Requests made more than 7 days after purchase
  • Abuse of the Service (spam, fraud, violation of Terms)
  • Repeated refund requests from the same user

7. Failed or Duplicate Payments

If you were charged twice or your payment failed but was still deducted, contact us immediately. We will investigate and issue a full refund within 5-7 business days.

8. Credits Never Expire

Your credits never expire — so you don't need to rush to use them. If you decide not to use them, you can still request a refund within the 7-day window.
