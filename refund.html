Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Free Features — No Payment Required

InvoiceFollow's core features are completely free and require no payment:

  • Invoice tracking (unlimited)
  • Promise tracking and timeline
  • Today's Action dashboard
  • Template-based WhatsApp and email reminders
  • Demand letter generator (template-based)
  • CSV import/export and Google Sheets import
  • Call log, deposit tracking, and all other non-AI features
  • Multi-device sync (after signup)

Since no payment is required for these features, there is nothing to refund.

2. Free AI Credits

Every user receives free AI credits automatically:

  • 10 credits as a guest (no signup required)
  • +15 credits upon signup (25 total)

Free credits are not refundable as they carry no monetary value. Unused free credits do not convert to cash or transfer between accounts.

3. Purchased AI Credits

If you purchase AI credits (e.g., ₹50 for 50 credits), the following refund policy applies:

  • Unused credits: Full refund within 7 days of purchase, provided none of the purchased credits have been used.
  • Partially used: Refund issued on the unused portion, prorated. For example, if you bought 50 credits and used 10, you can request a refund on the remaining 40 credits within 7 days.
  • Fully used: No refund once all purchased credits have been consumed.
  • After 7 days: No refunds will be issued after the 7-day window, regardless of usage.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at invoicefollow@gmail.com with:

  • Your registered email address
  • Razorpay payment ID or transaction reference
  • Date of purchase
  • Reason for refund (optional but helpful)

We process refunds within 5-7 business days of approval.

5. Razorpay Processing

All credit purchases are processed by Razorpay, our payment partner. Refunds are credited to the original payment method. Timelines may vary based on your bank or card issuer.

6. Failed or Duplicate Payments

If you were charged twice or your payment failed but was still deducted from your account, contact us immediately with the transaction ID. We investigate and issue a full refund within 5-7 business days.

7. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

If you use your own API key from a third-party AI provider (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, or OpenRouter), no payment is made to InvoiceFollow for AI tasks. Any costs incurred with your API provider are between you and that provider. We have no visibility, control, or responsibility for those charges.

8. No Subscription Model

InvoiceFollow does not have a subscription model. There is no recurring billing, no auto-renewal, and no cancellation process. You only pay once for credits if you choose to — nothing else.

9. Cancellation

There is nothing to cancel on InvoiceFollow. You can stop using the service at any time. Credits already purchased remain in your account and do not expire. You can export your data as CSV at any time before or after stopping use.
