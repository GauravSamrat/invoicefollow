Frequently asked

Yeh free hai ya paid?

Free plan mein 5 invoices tak track kar sakte ho. Uske baad ₹499/month Pro plan hai. Koi credit card nahi chahiye start karne ke liye.

Mera data kahan store hota hai?

Tumhare browser ke localStorage mein. Hum apne servers pe kuch store nahi karte. Isliye tumhara data sirf tumhare paas hai — aur tum kabhi bhi CSV export kar sakte ho.

WhatsApp automatically send karta hai?

Nahi. Hum message draft karte hain, phir WhatsApp open karte hain. Tum "Send" dabao — woh manual step hai by design. Automation ka option hai Pro plan mein, with consent.

Kya yeh accounting software replace karta hai?

Nahi. Tumhara accounting software ledger hold karta hai. InvoiceFollow woh track karta hai jo accounting software miss karta hai — conversations, promises, aur follow-ups.

Kya main apna data export kar sakta hoon?

Haan. Ek click mein CSV export. Sab invoices, clients, promises — sab kuch. Data tumhara hai, tumhare control mein hai.

Kya refund milta hai?

Haan. 7-day money-back guarantee. Bina koi sawaal poochhe. Just email karo aur refund ho jayega.
