Frequently asked

Is it really free?

Yes — tracking, reminders (templates), timeline, CSV import/export — everything is free forever, unlimited, no credit card. AI-personalized messages are optional and use a credits system.

How do AI credits work?

You get 10 free credits as a guest and +15 more when you sign up. Each AI-generated message (like a personalized reminder or demand letter) costs 1 credit. Templates are always free.

Can I use AI without buying credits?

Yes — bring your own API key from Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI, or others. Groq and Gemini offer generous free tiers. Your key stays in your browser; we never see it.

Do I need to sign up?

No — you can use InvoiceFollow as a guest with 10 free credits. Sign up only if you want multi-device sync, 15 bonus credits, and to save your data.

Where is my data stored?

Your invoices stay in your browser (guest mode) or in your private account (signed-in). API keys never leave your browser. We never see your keys or your client data.

What if I run out of credits?

Three options: (1) keep using free templates, (2) buy more credits (₹50 = 50 credits), or (3) add your own API key for unlimited AI. You choose — nothing is forced.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit purchases go through Razorpay — all major cards, UPI, netbanking. Free features never require payment.

Can I cancel anytime?

There's nothing to cancel — no subscription. Credits never expire. You can export everything as CSV anytime.
