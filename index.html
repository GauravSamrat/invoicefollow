Frequently asked

How do credits work?

You buy credits once and use them whenever you need. Each task — scanning an invoice, or sending a reminder — costs 3 credits. Credits never expire, so you can use them at your own pace.

What is a "task"?

A task is any AI-powered action in InvoiceFollow. For example: scanning an invoice from a photo, or generating a payment reminder email. Each task uses 3 credits.

Do credits expire?

No. Your credits never expire. Buy once, use whenever. No monthly commitment required.

Why should I choose Pro over credits?

Pro is for high-volume users. If you process 20+ tasks per month, Pro at ₹499/month gives you unlimited credits and priority support — usually cheaper than buying credits repeatedly.

Is there a refund policy?

Yes. Unused credits can be refunded within 7 days of purchase. See our Refund Policy for full details.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and netbanking — powered by Razorpay. Payments are processed securely.

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored securely in your account. You can access it from any device by signing in. You can also export everything as CSV anytime.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There's no lock-in. Credits can be used anytime. Pro subscription can be cancelled with one click — no cancellation fees.
